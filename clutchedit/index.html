What's inside

Sample management without the INI headaches

ClutchEdit is an editor and sample manager for the WMD Clutch module.

  • Cross platform (Windows and macOS)
  • Drag and drop a variety of sample formats, which will be converted to the correct format for Clutch
  • Audition samples
  • Edit Clutch settings from your computer without having to worry about messing up the HIHAT.INI file
  • All edits are non-destructive until you actually save the changes, and can be reverted while you are editing
  • v1.0.0 — Initial release
